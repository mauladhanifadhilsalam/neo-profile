In July 2024 I joined PT AMA Salam Indonesia, to work on the back end of a travel-insurance platform built on the principles of Sharia.<Sidenote n={1}>Sharia-compliant insurance is commonly called <i lang="ar-Latn">takaful</i>: participants pool contributions to help one another, and the operator manages the fund rather than selling risk.</Sidenote> My first work there was to refactor an existing codebase from Express.js to NestJS, and to help carry the architecture toward microservices.

Marx and Engels wrote that under modern conditions all that is solid melts into air. Software architecture knows the feeling. A codebase that seemed fixed becomes, under a refactor, liquid again for a while: routes, handlers and helpers are taken apart and set again as modules, providers and services. The art is to let the structure melt without letting the behaviour go with it.

Note III tells that story at more length.
