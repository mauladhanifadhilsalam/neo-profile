At the end of 2023 I began to take work from a second house, ByteLogic Inovation, alongside the first. Two pieces of that work deserve a record.

The first was for the Fitness Trainer Academy, an e-learning platform built on Laravel. Its course material lived in PDF e-books, and a PDF can be read but not answered. I turned that content into web pages with input forms and review notes, so that a lesson could be worked through on the page rather than merely read.

In the second I had the main hand: an All-Access coupon subscription, also in Laravel, paid for through the API of Flip.<Sidenote n={1}>Flip is an Indonesian payment service. The subscription took its payments through Flip’s API.</Sidenote> Payment code is unforgiving in a particular way. Every path through it ends in someone’s money, so every path has to be accounted for, including the ones where the gateway answers late, or not at all.

Marx’s eleventh thesis is a reproach to philosophers. It is also a fair description of commissioned work: nobody pays you to interpret their problem. They pay you to change it.
