In October 2024 my title changed from Software Engineer to Full Stack Developer, and the work widened to match: no longer the back end alone, but the platform from end to end. Dmitri Karamazov complains that man is too broad, and that he would have him narrower. A full-stack developer has no such luxury.

Alongside that work I have been turning toward multi-agent systems, in which several programs reason and act in concert, and toward development for Android and iOS. A Note on the first, <i>Of Polyphony</i>, is in press. From 2022 to 2026 I also studied computer software engineering at Institut Teknologi dan Bisnis Swadharma.

This chapter is still being written. Its particulars will be set down as the volume goes on.
