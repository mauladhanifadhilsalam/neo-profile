In January 2021 I was a pupil at SMK Wikrama Bogor, where I studied computer software engineering, and I went to Kejar.id as an intern.<Sidenote n={1}>An SMK (<i lang="id">sekolah menengah kejuruan</i>) is an Indonesian vocational upper-secondary school. A placement in industry is part of the course.</Sidenote> Kejar.id builds web software for Indonesian schools, and for six months I worked beside its developers on that platform: for the schools, for their pupils, and for the teachers who teach them.

It was the first time my code met real users. I helped design and build parts of the platform, made its pages responsive so they would serve a phone as well as a desk, and learned the languages of the trade properly: PHP, JavaScript, and above all the Laravel framework. I also learned the less visible lesson, that a codebase shared by a team needs agreed practice more than it needs cleverness.

The year was full in other ways. I scored 985 on the TOEIC, among the ten best results in a national programme of the Ministry of Education, and I reached the finals of the ITechno Cup, with a web app called Lawancov.id, and of the Battle of Technology at PINGFEST.

Nietzsche gave <cite>Ecce Homo</cite> the subtitle <i>How One Becomes What One Is</i>. Looking back, this was where the becoming started.
